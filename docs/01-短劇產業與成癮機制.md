# 01 · 短劇產業與成癮機制

> **English summary**
> Hongguo (ByteDance) made short drama free and ad-funded (IAA): users watch a 5-second ad instead
> of paying per episode, and the platform shares ad revenue with rights holders. The model removes
> the payment decision but keeps the time cost, and it is paired with 1–2 minute episodes, a
> three-second hook, a reversal every 15 seconds, a cliffhanger at every episode end and an
> autoplay feed. Addiction is the product of four interlocking layers: content structure, platform
> mechanics, psychological compensation and commercial incentives. On "does plot matter?": ad spend
> sets the floor of visibility, content sets the ceiling of retention — neither can be reduced to a
> single percentage with public data. The Fanqie novel pipeline industrialises this by pre-filtering
> IP with reading data (6,000+ titles in adaptation, 64-day turnaround), at the cost of
> homogenisation.

> 這一章回答兩個問題：**紅果短劇為什麼這麼吸引人、甚至讓人成癮？** 以及 **「劇情」對一個作品的成功到底有多重要？**
> 所有數字均標注來源與年份；行業數據存在「全市場／核心市場」「App／去重用戶」等口徑差異，已逐項標示。媒體測算而官方未確認者，一律寫「媒體測算」。

## 1. 紅果是什麼：一個把「注意力」變成廣告收入的機器

**紅果短劇**由抖音集團於 2023 年 5 月推出、8 月上線獨立 App。它的模式與早期小程序短劇完全不同：

| 維度 | 紅果短劇 | 抖音站內短劇 | 微信小程序短劇 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 變現 | **IAA 廣告解鎖**（看 5 秒廣告解鎖內容） | IAP 付費解鎖 | IAP 前幾集免費、後續付費 |
| 流量 | 抖音導流 + 演算法 | 抖音資訊流 | 微信社交分發 |
| IP | **番茄小說** | 番茄 + 外部 | 外部承製 |
| 用戶成本 | 0 元 | 按集付費 | 按集付費 |

關鍵細節：紅果**不設會員費、不逐集解鎖**，用戶看 5 秒以上強制廣告即可繼續，平台再按分賬把廣告收入分給版權方（[經濟觀察報，2026](https://m.eeo.com.cn/2026/0928/1050702.shtml)）。這把「付費決策」從每一集移除，只剩「時間成本」——這是成癮結構的第一塊骨牌。

**番茄系生態**：番茄小說＝IP 上游；番茄短劇／紅果＝播出變現；抖音＝引流分發；巨量引擎＝廣告投流。2025 年紅果合併抖音短劇、成立「短劇版權中心」（[36氪，2025](https://www.36kr.com/p/3571055911861123)）。

**IP 管線的工業化程度**（這是理解「劇情重不重要」的關鍵）：
- 番茄以「免費閱讀 + 演算法」累積點擊、完讀、追更數據，形成**選題漏斗**；
- 製作方圍繞已驗證 IP 做「拆結構、縮體量、強化爽點」改編；
- 截至 2025 年 10 月，番茄 IP 改編授權收入較 2024 年全年增長 **800%**，逾 **6,000 部**作品進入改編流程（[36氪，2025](https://www.36kr.com/p/3571055911861123)）；
- 全年 6,700 部被改編、3,600 部上架，簽約作家逾 60 萬、版權庫逾 150 萬部（[鈦媒體，2025](https://m.tmtpost.com/7811368.html)）；
- 《真千金她是學霸》從上線驗證到改編上線僅 **64 天**（[36氪，2025](https://www.36kr.com/p/3571055911861123)）。

> 換句話說：短劇的「劇情」不是靈感，而是**被數據預先篩選過的機率產品**。這會直接影響我們怎麼量化「劇情」。

## 2. 市場規模：兩個口徑，不要相加

| 指標 | 數值 | 年份 | 口徑／來源 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 市場規模 | 36.8 億元 | 2021 | 白皮書 |
| 市場規模 | 504.4 億元，首超電影票房 | 2024 | DataEye 白皮書 |
| 市場規模 | **突破 1,000 億元** | 2025 | 廣電總局「全市場」口徑 |
| 核心市場 | 634.3 億元；免費短劇 533 億元（+113%） | 2025 | 第三方核心市場口徑 |
| 投流規模 | 約 330 億元；2025 預估近 400 億元 | 2024 | DataEye |
| IAA 規模 | 約 250 億元；2025 預估 350 億元 | 2024 | DataEye |
| IAA 佔比 | **71%**（IAP 約 26%） | 2025–26 | WETRUE／電影界 |
| 用戶規模 | 6.96 億人（2025）→ 8.5 億人、月活逾 6 億（2026） | 2025–26 | 白皮書、廣電總局 |
| 人均單日時長 | **120.5 分鐘**（2025 年 1–8 月，較 1 月 +25.9%） | 2025 | 白皮書 |

**紅果自身的成長曲線（第三方口徑）**：2024.11 MAU 1.4 億 → 2025.10 MAU 2.45 億／日均 2.01 小時 → 2026.01 DAU 破 1 億 → 2026.07 DAU 1.68 億（+107%）→ 2026.09 MAU 4.3 億／日均 151 分鐘（QuestMobile 等，[鈦媒體](https://m.tmtpost.com/7879565.html)、[電影界](https://www.dianyingjie.com.cn/2026/1011/406946.shtml)）。

## 3. 成癮機制：四層拆解

成癮不是單一原因，而是**內容結構 × 平台機制 × 心理補償 × 商業激勵**四層咬合。任何一層單獨存在都不會成癮。

### 3.1 內容結構層：把「敘事」壓縮成「情緒單位」

| 設計 | 具體做法 | 作用 |
|---|---|---|
| **規格** | 單集 1–2 分鐘、全劇 80–100 集 | 每次消費的「啟動成本」趨近於零 |
| **前 3 秒鉤子** | 第一集前數十秒拋出最強衝突／反差 | 決定 3 秒內是否划走；前 3 集決定生死 |
| **節奏密度** | 15 秒一反轉、30 秒一推進、每集末 10 秒留懸念 | 業者稱「一分鐘約三個大爆點」 |
| **付費／廣告卡點** | IAP 在情緒峰值中斷；IAA 在同一位置插廣告 | 把「最想知道結果」的瞬間變成變現點 |
| **爽點公式** | 贅婿、戰神、重生、逆襲、甜寵、真假千金 | 情緒回報可預期，降低理解成本 |

業內共識是「重要的不是短，是密度」（[投資界，2025](https://news.pedaily.cn/202512/558298.shtml)）。這也是為什麼短劇的「劇情」不能只用「故事好不好」來衡量——它衡量的是**單位時間內的情緒事件密度**。

### 3.2 平台機制層：把「選擇」與「結束」都移除

- **演算法資訊流 + 抖音導流 + 自動連播**：取消「選劇」成本，也取消「自然的結束點」。
- **零現金摩擦**：免費模式下每次點擊不付費，改用時間與注意力支付。
- **分賬反向激勵**：分賬按「有效時長 × 單價 × 係數」計算，品質沒有單獨計價項，內容方只能設法拉長觀看（[Longbridge，2026](https://longbridge.com/zh-HK/quote/CP20053.HK/news/303842292)）。
- **遊戲化激勵**：簽到、看劇、做任務攢金幣可提現，把看劇變成「有獲得感的日常習慣」，製造商業含金量較低的「激勵型 DAU」（[經濟觀察報，2026](https://m.eeo.com.cn/2026/0928/1050702.shtml)）。
- **監管已注意到這點**：2026 年 9 月《微短劇發展管理辦法》第 37 條明令不得使用「誘導用戶沉迷、過度消費」的算法模型（[國務院公報，2026](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13006/202609/content_7082247.html)）。

### 3.3 心理層：不是「多巴胺」那麼簡單

用戶結構是關鍵：**40 歲以上近五成、逾六成來自三線及以下城市**（艾瑞，轉引經濟觀察報）。學術研究以 SOC 框架與 I-PACE 成癮模型檢驗「社會隔離感 → 情感空虛／娛樂需求 → 沉迷」的路徑（[《新媒體研究》2025](https://wap.cnki.net/touch/web/Journal/Article/XMTJ202509020.html)）。

三個可操作的機制：
1. **懸念閉環**：每集結尾留下未閉合的張力，大腦傾向於「再看一集把它關掉」。
2. **低成本高頻回饋**：每一次反轉都是一次小型獎賞，頻率遠高於長劇。
3. **補償性陪伴**：對時間碎片化、社交稀缺的族群，短劇提供低門檻的情緒陪伴。

> 注意：網路上常說的「短劇讓多巴胺爆炸」缺乏腦神經層級的因果證據，**不宜過度宣稱**。我們在網站上只描述行為機制，不做醫學結論。

### 3.4 商業層：投流是一台「放大器」

- 成本結構：製作僅佔約 **7.5%**、投流 **82.5%**；流水 1,000 萬中投流吃掉 600–800 萬，版權方淨利常只剩流水 3–5%（[飛天影業，2026](https://feitianyingye.com/2026/0928/6805.shtml)）。
- 投流手自述「**三天定生死**」，ROI 未過 1 即放棄（[新京報，2024](https://m.bjnews.com.cn/detail/171134139819639.html)）。
- 學術研究直言投流已成「決定成敗的主導性環節」，並反向規訓選題與敘事（[《中國電視》2026](https://wap.cnki.net/touch/web/Journal/Article/ZGDD202603009.html)）。

## 4. 「劇情」到底重不重要？分層答案

這是整個產品最核心的方法論問題。**沒有一個單一百分比可以回答它**，因為權重隨商業模式變化。比較誠實的答案是：

> **投流決定「被看見的下限」，內容決定「留下來的上限」。**

### 支持「劇情重要」的證據

| 證據 | 說明 |
|---|---|
| 零投流爆款《持械入宋》 | 原著非頭部流量 IP，靠文本與歷史考究突圍（[搜狐，2026](https://www.sohu.com/a/1101203477_698063)） |
| 同 IP 多版本對照 | 《十八歲太奶奶駕到》是原著第 **7** 個改編版本，靠演員適配與製作出線（[鈦媒體，2025](https://m.tmtpost.com/7811368.html)） |
| IAA 模式下留存權重上升 | 收入綁定「有效播放時長」，留存比一次性點擊值錢；同一 IP 常授權數十家公司競跑，故事不再稀缺，差異化落在改編與製作（同上） |
| 口碑破圈案例 | 《家里家外》用四川方言與生活細節，3 天紅果觀看破 10 億（[央視，2025](https://news.cctv.cn/2025/04/04/ARTI7WV5IJAeBWKTBg4p1CaM250404.shtml)） |

### 支持「投流更重要」的證據

| 證據 | 說明 |
|---|---|
| 成本結構 82.5% | 投流是最大成本項，直接決定曝光量 |
| ROI 1.03–1.07、三天定生死 | 投流效率而非內容品質決定是否繼續加投 |
| IAP 小程序劇 | 前幾集免費、高潮處付費，轉化主要靠「卡點設計 + 投流精準度」 |
| 行業研究 | 投流「反向規訓選題與敘事」（《中國電視》2026） |

### 我們的立場（也是網站的量化設計依據）

**把「劇情」拆成可觀測的中介變數**，而不是一個籠統的權重：

```
劇情 → 前 3 秒鉤子 → 3 秒完播率
     → 情緒事件密度 → 單集完播率 / 追更率
     → 改編品質 → 口碑分布 / 二創量
     → 題材適配度 → 題材歷史爆率
```

這些中介變數在外部只能部分觀測（見第 6 節），所以我們的做法是：
1. **不宣稱「劇情佔 X%」**，而是給出「內容層 vs 機制層」的分解（v0.2 模型，見 [02-量化評分機制](02-量化評分機制.md)）。
2. **用同題材基準線做對照**：一部劇的「破圈能力」＝相對同題材的超出幅度，這在一定程度上控制了題材紅利。
3. **公開不確定性**：短劇資料是估算，就標 estimated，並給 ±5.5 的區間。

## 5. 番茄小說：把「爆款不確定性」變成「可計算的機率」

番茄之於短劇，就像亞馬遜之於出版：**用閱讀數據預先篩選供給**。

- **漏斗**：點擊 → 完讀 → 追更 → 付費意願，數據直接告訴製作方「哪個題材、哪個開局、哪種人設」有轉化。
- **壓縮週期**：64 天從驗證到上線。
- **規模化**：6,000+ 部進入改編流程，一年 3,600 部上架。
- **代價**：同質化、一本多拍、IP 極速消耗（[流媒體網，2025](https://vv.lmtw.com/mzs/content/detail/id/248723)、[鈦媒體，2025](https://m.tmtpost.com/7811368.html)）。

對我們網站的意義：**「IP 來源」與「改編版本數」是兩個可公開觀測、且對爆款有預測力的特徵**。v0.2 模型應把它們納入內容層（`ip_source`、`adaptation_count`）。

## 6. 哪些數據外部可得？（決定網站能做多深）

| 類別 | 具體數據 | 可得性 |
|---|---|---|
| **免費可得** | 紅果／抖音／快手 App 內榜單與熱度值、上線日、集數、題材標籤、平台分賬月報 | 🟢 高（但只能人工或低頻取得） |
| **半可得（付費）** | DataEye 劇查查（30 元／月起）、WETRUE 熱度與播放量、雲合有效播放與集均 V7／V30、QuestMobile MAU／DAU／時長 | 🟡 中（企業訂閱） |
| **拿不到** | 真實分賬金額、eCPM／CPM、完播率與付費轉化、留存曲線、演算法權重、IP 授權金 | 🔴 低 |

**代理指標建議**（MVP 已在用）：
- 自然熱度 → 7 日評論／彈幕增長斜率（買量帶來播放，但未必帶來討論）
- 長尾 → 上架 7 日後流量佔比，或「上架天數 vs 累積播放」的 log-log 斜率
- 投流強度 → 素材投放數、是否有首頁推薦位（ordinal）
- 完播 → 彈幕密度／分鐘、劇集留存討論

## 9. 給產品的三個結論

1. **短劇的「好看」是可以拆的**：鉤子、密度、反轉、情緒回報、題材紅利，每一項都能找到代理指標。
2. **不要試圖證明「劇情佔幾成」**：公開資料不足以做因果歸因。誠實的做法是分層展示 + 標記不確定性 + 提供研究者可檢驗的資料。
3. **紅果模式（IAA）改變了內容權重**：當收入綁定「有效時長」，「留得住」比「點得開」更重要——這正是「口碑／續航」維度在 2025 年後應該加權的原因。

## English (full version)

> This chapter answers two questions: **why is Hongguo short drama so engaging — even addictive?** and **how much does "plot" actually matter to a title's success?**
> Every number carries a source and a year. Industry data differ in definition ("whole market" vs. "core market", "app users" vs. "deduplicated users"), so we flag each figure individually. Figures calculated by media but not confirmed officially are always labelled "media estimate".

### 1. What Hongguo is: a machine that turns attention into ad revenue

**Hongguo Short Drama** was launched by Douyin (ByteDance) in May 2023, with a standalone app following in August. Its model is completely different from the early mini-program short dramas:

| Dimension | Hongguo Short Drama | Douyin in-app short drama | WeChat mini-program short drama |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monetisation | **IAA ad-unlock** (watch a 5-second ad to unlock content) | IAP paid unlock | IAP: first few episodes free, rest paid |
| Traffic | Douyin referral + algorithm | Douyin feed | WeChat social distribution |
| IP | **Fanqie Novel** | Fanqie + external | Externally produced |
| User cost | Free | Pay per episode | Pay per episode |

Key detail: Hongguo **charges no membership fee and does not unlock episode by episode**. Users simply watch a forced ad of 5 seconds or more to continue, and the platform then shares the ad revenue with rights holders under its revenue-share arrangement ([Economic Observer, 2026](https://m.eeo.com.cn/2026/0928/1050702.shtml)). That removes the "payment decision" from every episode and leaves only the "time cost" — the first domino in the addiction structure.

**The Fanqie ecosystem**: Fanqie Novel = upstream IP; Fanqie Short Drama / Hongguo = broadcast and monetisation; Douyin = traffic acquisition and distribution; Ocean Engine = ad buying. In 2025 Hongguo merged Douyin's short drama operation and set up a "short drama copyright centre" ([36Kr, 2025](https://www.36kr.com/p/3571055911861123)).

**How industrialised the IP pipeline is** (this is the key to understanding "does plot matter"):
- Fanqie accumulates clicks, completion and follow-on reading data through "free reading + algorithm", forming a **topic-selection funnel**;
- Producers adapt already-validated IP with a repeatable formula: "unpack the structure, shrink the length, amplify the payoff";
- As of October 2025, Fanqie's IP adaptation licensing revenue was up **800%** on full-year 2024, with more than **6,000** titles in the adaptation pipeline ([36Kr, 2025](https://www.36kr.com/p/3571055911861123));
- 6,700 titles were adapted and 3,600 released during the year, with more than 600,000 (60 萬) contracted authors and a catalogue of over 1.5 million (150 萬) titles ([TMTPost, 2025](https://m.tmtpost.com/7811368.html));
- *Zhen Qianjin Ta Shi Xueba* (《真千金她是學霸》) went from launch validation to adapted release in just **64 days** ([36Kr, 2025](https://www.36kr.com/p/3571055911861123)).

> In other words: the "plot" of a short drama is not inspiration — it is **a probability product pre-filtered by data**. That directly shapes how we quantify "plot".

### 3. The addiction mechanism: four layers

Addiction is not a single cause; it is the interlocking of **content structure × platform mechanics × psychological compensation × commercial incentives**. No single layer on its own creates addiction.

#### 3.1 Content structure: compressing "narrative" into "emotional units"

| Design | Implementation | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | 1–2 minutes per episode, 80–100 episodes per series | The "start-up cost" of each session approaches zero |
| **Three-second hook** | The strongest conflict / contrast is thrown out in the first few dozen seconds of episode one | Decides whether viewers swipe away within 3 seconds; the first 3 episodes decide survival |
| **Rhythm density** | A reversal every 15 seconds, a plot push every 30 seconds, a cliffhanger in the last 10 seconds of each episode | The industry calls it "about three major beats per minute" |
| **Paywall / ad break points** | IAP interrupts at an emotional peak; IAA inserts an ad at the same spot | Turns the moment "I most want to know the outcome" into the monetisation point |
| **Payoff formulas** | Son-in-law revenge, war god, rebirth, counter-attack, sweet romance, real/fake heiress | Emotional rewards are predictable and comprehension cost is low |

The industry consensus is that "what matters is not brevity, it is density" ([PEdaily, 2025](https://news.pedaily.cn/202512/558298.shtml)). That is also why a short drama's "plot" cannot be judged merely by "is the story good" — it measures **emotional event density per unit of time**.

#### 3.2 Platform mechanics: removing both "choice" and "ending"

- **Algorithmic feed + Douyin referral + autoplay**: removes the cost of choosing a show, and also removes the natural ending point.
- **Zero cash friction**: in the free model each click costs no money; users pay with time and attention instead.
- **Revenue-share counter-incentives**: revenue share is calculated as "valid watch time × unit price × coefficient"; quality has no line item of its own, so content owners can only try to extend viewing ([Longbridge, 2026](https://longbridge.com/zh-HK/quote/CP20053.HK/news/303842292)).
- **Gamified incentives**: check-ins, watching shows and completing tasks accumulate coins that can be cashed out, turning viewing into "a daily habit that feels rewarding" and manufacturing "incentive-driven DAU" of low commercial value ([Economic Observer, 2026](https://m.eeo.com.cn/2026/0928/1050702.shtml)).
- **Regulators have noticed**: in September 2026, Article 37 of the *Measures for the Administration of the Development of Micro Short Dramas* explicitly bans algorithm models that "induce users into addiction or excessive consumption" ([State Council Gazette, 2026](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_13006/202609/content_7082247.html)).

#### 3.3 Psychology: more than "dopamine"

The user structure is the key: **nearly 50% are 40 or older, and over 60% come from tier-3 cities and below** (iResearch, cited by Economic Observer). Academic work uses the SOC framework and the I-PACE addiction model to test the path "social isolation → emotional emptiness / entertainment need → excessive use" ([*New Media Research*, 2025](https://wap.cnki.net/touch/web/Journal/Article/XMTJ202509020.html)).

Three actionable mechanisms:
1. **Unclosed suspense loops**: each episode ends with unresolved tension, and the brain tends to "watch one more to close it".
2. **Low-cost, high-frequency reward**: every reversal is a small reward, at a far higher frequency than long-form drama.
3. **Compensatory companionship**: for people with fragmented time and scarce social contact, short drama offers low-threshold emotional company.

> Note: the popular claim that "short drama makes dopamine explode" lacks causal evidence at the neuroscience level, so **we should not overclaim**. On the site we describe behavioural mechanisms only, and make no medical claims.

#### 3.4 Commercial layer: ad buying is an amplifier

- Cost structure: production accounts for only about **7.5%**, ad buying **82.5%**; of RMB 10 million (1,000 萬) in gross revenue, ad buying consumes RMB 6–8 million, and rights holders often net only 3–5% of gross revenue ([Feitian Film, 2026](https://feitianyingye.com/2026/0928/6805.shtml)).
- A media buyer's own account: "**three days decide life or death**"; if ROI does not clear 1, the title is dropped ([Beijing News, 2024](https://m.bjnews.com.cn/detail/171134139819639.html)).
- Academic research states bluntly that ad buying has become "the dominant stage that decides success or failure", and that it disciplines topic selection and narrative in reverse ([*China Television*, 2026](https://wap.cnki.net/touch/web/Journal/Article/ZGDD202603009.html)).

### 4. Does "plot" matter? A layered answer

This is the core methodological question of the whole product. **No single percentage can answer it**, because the weights shift with the business model. The more honest answer is:

> **Ad buying sets the floor of being seen; content sets the ceiling of being kept.**

#### Evidence that "plot matters"

| Evidence | What it shows |
|---|---|
| The zero-ad-spend hit *Chi Xie Ru Song* (《持械入宋》) | The source novel was not a top-traffic IP; it broke through on writing and historical detail ([Sohu, 2026](https://www.sohu.com/a/1101203477_698063)) |
| Same IP, multiple versions | *Shiba Sui Tai Nainai Jia Dao* (《十八歲太奶奶駕到》) was the **7th** adaptation of the source novel and stood out on casting fit and production ([TMTPost, 2025](https://m.tmtpost.com/7811368.html)) |
| Retention weighs more under IAA | Revenue is tied to "valid watch time", so retention is worth more than a one-off click; the same IP is often licensed to dozens of companies racing in parallel, so story is no longer scarce and differentiation falls to adaptation and production (same source) |
| Word-of-mouth breakout | *Jia Li Jia Wai* (《家里家外》) used Sichuan dialect and everyday detail to pass 1 billion (10 億) views on Hongguo in 3 days ([CCTV, 2025](https://news.cctv.cn/2025/04/04/ARTI7WV5IJAeBWKTBg4p1CaM250404.shtml)) |

#### Evidence that "ad buying matters more"

| Evidence | What it shows |
|---|---|
| An 82.5% cost structure | Ad buying is the largest cost item and directly determines exposure |
| ROI 1.03–1.07, three days decide survival | Ad-buying efficiency, not content quality, decides whether spend continues |
| IAP mini-program dramas | First episodes free, payment at the climax; conversion depends mainly on "break-point design + ad-targeting precision" |
| Industry research | Ad buying "disciplines topic selection and narrative in reverse" (*China Television*, 2026) |

#### Our position (and the basis of the site's quantitative design)

**Break "plot" into observable mediator variables** instead of one vague weight:

```
plot → three-second hook → 3-second completion rate
     → emotional event density → per-episode completion / follow-on rate
     → adaptation quality → rating distribution / fan-edit volume
     → genre fit → historical breakout rate for the genre
```

These mediators can only be partly observed externally (see §6), so our approach is:
1. **Never claim "plot accounts for X%"**; instead we publish a content-layer vs. mechanism-layer decomposition (v0.2 model, see [02-Quantitative scoring](02-量化評分機制.md)).
2. **Use genre baselines as the control**: a title's "breakout ability" = how far it exceeds its own genre, which controls for genre tailwind to some extent.
3. **Publish uncertainty**: short-drama data is estimated, so label it `estimated` and give a ±5.5 interval.

### 5. Fanqie Novel: turning "hit uncertainty" into "calculable probability"

Fanqie is to short drama what Amazon is to publishing: **it pre-filters supply with reading data**.

- **Funnel**: click → completion → follow-on reading → willingness to pay; the data tells producers directly which topic, which opening and which character type convert.
- **Compressed cycles**: 64 days from validation to release.
- **Scale**: 6,000+ titles in the adaptation pipeline, 3,600 released in a year.
- **Cost**: homogenisation, one book filmed many times, extremely fast IP depletion ([Lmtw, 2025](https://vv.lmtw.com/mzs/content/detail/id/248723), [TMTPost, 2025](https://m.tmtpost.com/7811368.html)).

What this means for the site: **"IP source" and "number of adaptations" are two publicly observable features that predict breakouts**. The v0.2 model should bring them into the content layer (`ip_source`, `adaptation_count`).

### 6. What data is available externally? (this caps how deep the site can go)

| Category | Specific data | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| **Freely available** | In-app charts and heat values from Hongguo / Douyin / Kuaishou, release dates, episode counts, genre tags, platform monthly revenue-share reports | 🟢 High (but only manually or at low frequency) |
| **Semi-available (paid)** | DataEye Juchacha (from RMB 30/month), WETRUE heat and play counts, Yunhe valid plays and per-episode V7/V30, QuestMobile MAU/DAU/time spent | 🟡 Medium (enterprise subscriptions) |
| **Unavailable** | Real revenue-share amounts, eCPM/CPM, completion and paid conversion, retention curves, algorithm weights, IP licensing fees | 🔴 Low |

**Proxy metrics (already used in the MVP)**:
- Organic heat → 7-day slope of comment / danmaku growth (paid traffic brings plays, but not necessarily discussion)
- Long tail → share of traffic 7+ days after release, or the log-log slope of "days live vs. cumulative plays"
- Ad-buying intensity → number of creative assets in rotation, whether there is a home-page slot (ordinal)
- Completion → danmaku density per minute, discussion of per-episode retention

### 7. Regulatory timeline (affects data availability and content selection)

| Date | Document | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| 2024.06 | *Latest Working Notice on Micro Short Drama Filing* | Titles may not go online without review and filing; filing is tiered by investment amount |
| 2025.02 | *Notice on Further Coordinating Development and Security to Promote the Healthy and Prosperous Development of the Online Micro Short Drama Industry* | Three tiers — "key / ordinary / other"; home-page, first-screen and first-recommendation slots must be re-checked by NRTA |
| 2026.09 | *Measures for the Administration of the Development of Micro Short Dramas* (Order No. 16) | Defines a micro short drama as under 20 minutes per episode; tiered filing and distribution licences; AI-generated content must be labelled; **no algorithm models that induce addiction**; paid content must be clearly disclosed |

**Impact**: compliance costs rise, supply shifts from "volume" to "quality", and the revenue-share coefficient for AI-generated drama is adjusted across several rounds. For a data site, **"filing number" and "licence number" will become publicly verifiable fields**, which can be turned into "compliance labels".

| Player | 2025 performance |

| ReelShort | Full-year revenue close to RMB 4.3 billion (43 億元人民幣), with the US accounting for nearly 70% |

**What this means for the site**: if we only cover Chinese short drama, the ceiling is a regional market; if we bring ReelShort and DramaBox into the same index, we have a shot at a "global short-drama standard". That requires English content and overseas data sources (Sensor Tower / Appfigures) and is the focus of Phase 2.

### 9. Three conclusions for the product

1. **A short drama's "watchability" can be decomposed**: hook, density, reversal, emotional reward, genre tailwind — each has a findable proxy metric.
2. **Do not try to prove "plot accounts for X%"**: public data cannot support causal attribution. The honest approach is layered display + labelled uncertainty + data that researchers can test.
3. **The Hongguo model (IAA) changed content weights**: when revenue is tied to "valid watch time", "being kept" matters more than "being opened" — which is exactly why the "word of mouth / longevity" dimension should carry more weight after 2025.
