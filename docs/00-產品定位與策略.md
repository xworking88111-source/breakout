# 00 · 產品定位與策略

> **English summary**
> Breakout is a cross-media breakout index, not another streaming or review site: a data terminal
> where short dramas, anime, novels and films share one explainable ruler. Three audiences — casual
> viewers deciding what to watch, data workers needing comparable and exportable numbers, and
> researchers needing citable snapshots — all want the same thing: a trustworthy *relative
> position*. IMDb's four structural gaps (no explanation, no short drama, no cross-media
> comparability, no citable snapshots) define the wedge. The product has three layers: entry
> (charts), explanation (title pages), trust (methodology + research mode). The business model must
> never sell rankings; authority is the moat.

## 1. 一句話

**Breakout 破圈** 是一個「跨媒介爆款數據站」：把短劇、動漫、小說、電影放進同一套可解釋的指數，用一屏告訴你「現在什麼厲害、為什麼」，並把方法論與原始資料開放給研究者。

不是另一個串流平台，也不是另一個豆瓣。是**內容市場的 Bloomberg 終端（簡化版）**：榜單是入口，解釋是產品，方法論是護城河。

---

## 2. 客群與工作（Jobs To Be Done）

| 客群 | 他們的工作 | 現在怎麼解 | 痛點 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **想知道什麼好看的人**（最大盤） | 「今晚兩小時，看什麼不踩雷？」 | 豆瓣／IMDb／朋友推薦／平台首頁 | 選擇成本高、評分通膨、平台首頁是廣告位 |
| **數據工作者**（增長引擎） | 「把內容熱度變成可比較、可匯出的資料」 | 手動截榜單、Excel、付費 SaaS | 口徑不一、無歷史快照、無法引用 |
| **研究者**（權威來源） | 「短劇爆款的因素是什麼？可不可以重現？」 | 訪談、個案、付費報告 | 沒有公開、可驗證、帶版本號的資料集 |

**核心洞察**：這三類人其實要同一件事——**一個可信的「相對位置」**。一般觀眾要「這部在同類裡排第幾」，數據工作者要「這個排名的口徑與誤差」，研究者要「這個排名是怎麼算出來、能不能引用」。所以產品的第一性不是「更多內容」，而是**可比較性 + 可解釋性 + 可追溯性**。

---

## 3. 定位宣言

> 給想要快速判斷「什麼值得看」的人與需要引用內容數據的研究者，
> **Breakout** 是一個跨媒介的爆款指數與解釋層。
> 有別於 IMDb 只給分數、豆瓣只給中文文藝圈口碑、DataEye 只給中國短劇付費榜，
> 我們**用同一把尺量不同媒介、把每個分數拆成可解釋的因素、並公開公式與資料截止日**。

---

## 4. 「超越 IMDb」不是口號，是四個結構性缺口

IMDb 很強，但它的設計目標是「電影資料庫 + 評分」，不是「幫你決定看什麼」。它的四個缺口正好是我們的切入點：

| IMDb 的缺口 | 為什麼存在 | Breakout 的做法 |
|---|---|---|
| **只給分數，不給原因** | 評分是使用者行為的聚合，沒有內容層與機制層資料 | 五維拆解 + 「為什麼火」瀑布圖 + 編輯註記與來源 |
| **短劇完全缺席** | 短劇是 2023 年後的新物種，且主要在中國／東南亞 | 以短劇為第一賽道，建立產業標準（這是最大差異化） |
| **跨媒介不可比** | 電影／影集／動畫分開計分，量級不同 | 以「同類相對位置」為語義的 0–100 絕對刻度 |
| **研究者不可引用** | 無公開公式、無資料快照、無 API | 版本化快照、區間估計、CSV、方法論頁、引用格式 |

**但要誠實**：短期內我們不可能在「電影資料庫完整性」上超越 IMDb（那是 30 年、數百萬條目的護城河）。合理的目標是：

1. **在短劇賽道**成為事實標準（目前全球沒有公認的短劇評分體系）。
2. **在「可解釋性」**上超越所有現有站點（IMDb、豆瓣、Bangumi 都做不到）。
3. **在華語＋國際雙語**上建立橋樑（繁中市場長期缺乏國際級數據站）。

## 5. 產品三層架構

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  L1 入口層：榜單 + 推薦                        │  一般使用者，一屏解決
│  今日焦點 / 正在爆 / 破圈榜 / 3 秒選片          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  L2 解釋層：作品詳情                            │  進階使用者，回答「為什麼」
│  五維雷達 / 瀑布圖 / 熱度曲線 / 相似作品 / 來源   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  L3 信任層：方法論 + 研究者模式                  │  數據工作者與研究者
│  公式 / 權重 / 限制 / 快照 / CSV / 引用 / API    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**關鍵設計**：L3 不是「關於我們」的裝飾頁，而是產品的差異化核心。每次 L1 顯示一個數字，都必須能在兩次點擊內到達 L3 的口徑說明。這叫「可解釋性契約」。

---

## 6. 針對「大陸 App 很雜亂」的解法

使用者原話是「功能很多很厲害但很雜亂」。拆開來看是三件事：

1. **推銷邏輯 vs 比較邏輯**：首頁是金剛區 + 活動彈窗 + 金幣任務，因為平台要的是停留與轉化，不是幫你決策。
2. **資訊層級過深**：榜單藏在三級頁，且預設是推廣位。
3. **廣告與內容難辨**：原生廣告混入資訊流。

我們的反向設計（已落實在 MVP）：

| 他們的問題 | 我們的原則 |
|---|---|
| 首頁入口爆炸 | **一屏一任務**：首頁只答「今天什麼值得看」 |
| 封面／海報搶眼但無資訊 | **數據先於封面**：分數、狀態、動能放第一行 |
| 金幣、簽到、任務 | **零任務、零彈窗**：不做成癮設計 |
| 榜單藏在深處 | **兩次點擊可達**：任何作品 ≤ 2 點擊；篩選寫進 URL |
| 數字沒有口徑 | **可解釋優先**：每個指標可點開看來源、樣本、截止日 |

還有一個容易被忽略的點：**「簡化」不是「減少功能」，而是「減少決策成本」**。所以我們保留篩選、比較、匯出這些「進階功能」，但把它們放在 L2/L3，讓一般使用者第一眼只看到三個數字：**分數、排名、為什麼**。

## 7. 北極星指標與護城河

**北極星**：每週「因為看到分數而做出觀看決定」的次數（可用 outbound click 到平台／預告 proxy）。

**二級指標**：
- 內容覆蓋：短劇 ≥ 500 部、動漫 ≥ 3,000 部、小說 ≥ 1,000 部
- 資料新鮮度：榜單类指标日更、作品頁週更
- 解釋使用率：詳情頁「為什麼火」區塊的展開率
- 研究者使用：CSV 下載數、引用數、API 呼叫數
- 雙語平衡：英文流量占比（目標 30%+）

**護城河（依可持續性排序）**：
1. **方法論與歷史快照**：越早開始記錄，越難被追上（時間是不可複製的資產）。
2. **短劇標準制定權**：如果業界開始引用「Breakout 指數」，就是標準。
3. **雙語內容對照**：華語作品的正確英譯、國際作品的繁中譯名，是苦工也是壁壘。
4. **研究者社群**：可引用的資料集會產生論文與報導，形成權威飛輪。

**不是護城河**：榜單本身（誰都能抄）、UI（會被模仿）、推薦演算法（資料不足時無效）。

## 8. 商業模式（建議）

| 模式 | 對象 | 價格假設 | 風險 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **免費 + 廣告** | 一般使用者 | CPM 低，但可維持 | 破壞簡潔體驗 → 建議只做「非侵入式」 |
| **Pro 訂閱** | 數據工作者 | US$8–12/月 | 需要歷史資料、匯出、警示 |
| **B2B 儀表板 / API** | 製作公司、平台、投資人 | US$500–5,000/月 | 需要穩定資料源與 SLA |
| **資料授權 / 報告** | 研究機構、媒體 | 按報告計價 | 需方法論權威性 |
| **聯盟導流** | OTT / 平台 | 按轉換 | 需揭露商業關係 |

**不可以做**：賣排名、賣評分、接受平台「優化」分數。一旦做了，L3 信任層就崩了，整個產品歸零。

## 9. 主要風險與對策

| 風險 | 等級 | 對策 |
|---|---|---|
| 資料合法性（爬蟲／榜單轉載） | 🔴 高 | 只用公開 API + 衍生統計；付費授權優先；不做全文／鏡像；見 docs/03 |
| 短劇資料不可得（平台封閉） | 🔴 高 | 多源代理指標（YouTube、App 榜、新聞稿）；先做「估算 + 透明標記」 |

## 12. 可以插入廣告收費嗎？

**技術上可以，但建議 MVP 階段不要；真的要放，必須遵守四條紅線。**

### 12.1 可行性

| 方案 | 門檻 | 預期收入（月流量 10 萬 PV 估算） | 風險 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AdSense | 需審核、需隱私政策 | 約 US$100–400 | 廣告品質不可控、可能出現低質廣告，破壞「簡潔」定位 |
| EthicalAds / Carbon Ads | 開發者／技術受眾導向 | 約 US$200–600 | 台灣／香港流量供給較少 |
| 直接贊助（平台／製作公司） | 需業務開發 | 單筆 US$500–5,000 | **利益衝突**：贊助商可能要求排名 |
| 聯盟導流（OTT 註冊／觀看） | 需平台合作 | 按轉換計 | 需明確揭露，且台灣可用平台有限 |

### 12.2 四條紅線（不可妥協）

1. **不賣排名、不賣分數**：一旦被發現，L3 信任層崩潰，產品歸零。
2. **廣告不進首屏、不進分數區**：首頁第一屏與作品分數周邊永遠無廣告。
3. **必須揭露**：贊助內容、聯盟連結一律標示；隱私政策寫清楚追蹤方式。
4. **不用侵入式格式**：不插頁、不彈窗、不自動播放影片——這正是我們批評大陸 App 的地方。

### 12.3 已預留的版位（程式碼）

- 位置：首頁底部、排行榜底部、作品詳情頁底部（皆在主要內容之後，不進首屏）。
- 開關：`app/src/config.ts` 的 `AD_SLOT.enabled`（預設 `false`，生產環境完全不渲染）。
- 元件：`app/src/components/AdSlot.tsx`，開發環境會顯示虛線佔位方便設計。
- 版位不影響版面高度（未啟用時 `display: none` 等級的條件渲染）。

### 12.4 建議

- **第一年**：不接廣告。用「免費 + 研究者引用 + 媒體報導」建立權威，這是唯一能超越 IMDb 的路徑。
- **第二年**：若流量穩定，接 1 個 EthicalAds 版位（頁尾上方），或做「贊助研究報告」。
- **長期**：B2B 資料 API／儀表板（US$500–5,000/月）的單位經濟遠優於展示型廣告，且不傷害使用者體驗。

> 記住：這個網站的價值是「幫使用者省時間」。任何讓你多停留 30 秒的廣告，都在稀釋這個價值。

## English (full version)

### 1. One-liner

**Breakout** is a cross-media hit-data site: it puts short drama, anime, novels and film on one explainable index, tells you on a single screen "what is strong right now, and why", and opens both the methodology and the raw data to researchers.

It is not another streaming platform, and not another Douban. It is **a (simplified) Bloomberg terminal for the content market**: charts are the entrance, explanation is the product, methodology is the moat.

### 2. Audience and jobs to be done

| Audience | Their job | How they solve it today | Pain point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Viewers who want to know what to watch** (largest pool) | "I have two hours tonight — what won't waste them?" | Douban / IMDb / friends / platform home page | High cost of choosing, rating inflation, home pages that are really ad slots |
| **Data workers** (growth engine) | "Turn content heat into comparable, exportable data" | Screenshotting charts by hand, Excel, paid SaaS | Inconsistent definitions, no historical snapshots, nothing citable |
| **Researchers** (source of authority) | "What drives a short-drama hit? Can it be reproduced?" | Interviews, case studies, paid reports | No public, verifiable, versioned dataset |

**Core insight**: all three groups actually want the same thing — **a trustworthy "relative position"**. Casual viewers want "where does this rank among its peers"; data workers want "what definitions and error bars sit behind that rank"; researchers want "how was it computed, and can I cite it". So the product's first principle is not "more content" but **comparability + explainability + traceability**.

### 3. Positioning statement

> For people who need to judge quickly what is worth watching, and for researchers who need citable content data,
> **Breakout** is a cross-media hit index and explanation layer.
> Unlike IMDb, which only gives scores, Douban, which only gives Chinese-language arts-and-culture buzz, and DataEye, which only gives paid China short-drama charts,
> we **measure different media with one ruler, break every score into explainable factors, and publish the formula and the data cutoff date**.

### 4. "Beat IMDb" is not a slogan — it is four structural gaps

IMDb is strong, but its design goal is "film database + ratings", not "help you decide what to watch". Its four gaps are exactly our entry points:

| IMDb's gap | Why it exists | Breakout's answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Scores without reasons** | Ratings are an aggregate of user behaviour; there is no content-level or mechanism-level data | Five-dimension decomposition + a "why it broke out" waterfall + editorial notes and sources |
| **Short drama entirely absent** | Short drama is a post-2023 species, and it lives mainly in China and Southeast Asia | Make short drama the first track and set the industry standard (our biggest differentiator) |
| **Cross-media not comparable** | Film / series / anime are scored separately, at different magnitudes | A 0–100 absolute scale whose meaning is "relative position among peers" |
| **Not citable by researchers** | No public formula, no data snapshots, no API | Versioned snapshots, interval estimates, CSV, methodology page, citation format |

**But let's be honest**: in the short term we cannot beat IMDb on "completeness of a film database" (that is a 30-year moat of millions of entries). Reasonable goals are:

1. Become the de facto standard **in the short-drama category** (there is currently no globally recognised short-drama rating system).
2. Beat every existing site **on explainability** (IMDb, Douban and Bangumi all fail here).
3. Build a bridge **between Chinese-language and international audiences** (the Traditional Chinese market has long lacked a world-class data site).

### 5. Three-layer product architecture

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  L1 Entry: charts + picks                                                  │  Casual viewers, solved in one screen
│  Today's spotlight / Breaking out / Breakout top 10 / 3-second picker      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  L2 Explanation: title pages                                               │  Power users, answering "why"
│  Five-dimension radar / waterfall / heat curve / similar titles / sources  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  L3 Trust: methodology + researcher mode                                   │  Data workers and researchers
│  Formula / weights / limits / snapshots / CSV / citation / API             │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**The key design decision**: L3 is not a decorative "About us" page; it is the product's differentiating core. Every number shown at L1 must be reachable from its L3 definition within two clicks. We call this the **explainability contract**.

### 6. The answer to "mainland apps are cluttered"

The user's own words were "lots of impressive features but very cluttered". Unpacked, that is three separate things:

1. **Promotion logic vs. comparison logic**: the home page is an icon grid + campaign pop-ups + coin tasks, because the platform wants dwell time and conversion, not your decision.
2. **Information hierarchy too deep**: charts are buried three levels down, and the default slots are paid placements.
3. **Ads indistinguishable from content**: native advertising is mixed into the feed.

Our counter-design (already shipped in the MVP):

| Their problem | Our principle |
|---|---|
| Exploding home-page entry points | **One task per screen**: the home page answers only "what is worth watching today" |
| Covers and posters dominate, but carry no information | **Data before artwork**: score, status and momentum sit on the first line |
| Coins, check-ins, tasks | **Zero tasks, zero pop-ups**: no addictive design |
| Charts buried deep | **Two clicks to anything**: any title in ≤ 2 clicks; filter state written into the URL |
| Numbers with no definition | **Explainability first**: every metric opens its source, sample and cutoff date |

One more easily missed point: **"simplify" does not mean "remove features"; it means "reduce decision cost"**. So we keep filters, comparison and export — the "advanced features" — but place them at L2/L3, so a casual viewer first sees only three numbers: **score, rank, why**.

### 7. North-star metric and moat

**North star**: the number of times per week a user makes a viewing decision because of a score seen on the site (proxied by outbound clicks to platforms / trailers).

**Secondary metrics**:
- Coverage: short drama ≥ 500 titles, anime ≥ 3,000, novels ≥ 1,000
- Data freshness: chart metrics refreshed daily, title pages weekly
- Explanation usage: expand rate of the "why it broke out" block on title pages
- Researcher usage: CSV downloads, citations, API calls
- Bilingual balance: share of English traffic (target 30%+)

**Moat (ordered by durability)**:
1. **Methodology and historical snapshots**: the earlier you start recording, the harder you are to catch (time is an asset no one can copy).
2. **Standard-setting power in short drama**: if the industry starts citing "the Breakout Index", that is the standard.
3. **Bilingual content mapping**: correct English titles for Chinese-language works, and Traditional Chinese titles for international works — tedious work, and a barrier.
4. **Researcher community**: a citable dataset generates papers and press, forming an authority flywheel.

**Not a moat**: the chart itself (anyone can copy it), the UI (it will be imitated), the recommendation algorithm (ineffective with thin data).

### 8. Business model (recommended)

| Model | Buyer | Price assumption | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free + ads** | Casual viewers | Low CPM but sustainable | Damages the clean experience → non-intrusive formats only |
| **Pro subscription** | Data workers | US$8–12/month | Needs history, export and alerts |
| **B2B dashboard / API** | Production companies, platforms, investors | US$500–5,000/month | Needs stable data sources and an SLA |
| **Data licensing / reports** | Research institutes, media | Priced per report | Requires methodological authority |
| **Affiliate referral** | OTTs / platforms | Per conversion | Requires disclosure of the commercial relationship |

**Off the table**: selling ranks, selling scores, accepting platform "optimisation" of scores. Do it once and the L3 trust layer collapses, taking the whole product with it.

### 9. Main risks and mitigations

| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Data legality (scraping / republishing charts) | 🔴 High | Public APIs + derived statistics only; licensed data first; no full text or mirrors; see docs/03 |
| Short-drama data unavailable (closed platforms) | 🔴 High | Multi-source proxies (YouTube, app charts, press releases); start with "estimated + transparent labelling" |
| Rating manipulation / paid chart positions | 🟠 Medium | Bayesian shrinkage, sample thresholds, distribution anomaly detection, a public exclusion log |
| Sample bias (AniList's English-speaking skew) | 🟠 Medium | State the bias; add Bangumi / MAL cross-checks |
| Commoditisation (everyone ships charts) | 🟠 Medium | Differentiate on explanation and methodology, not on the chart |
| Legal and political sensitivity (Chinese content) | 🟠 Medium | No political commentary; stay with data and industry analysis; regional compliance |
| Creator backlash (being scored) | 🟡 Low | Provide appeal and correction channels; separate scores from editorial picks |

### 12. Can we add advertising?

**Technically yes, but not during the MVP; if you do it, four red lines are non-negotiable.**

#### 12.1 Feasibility

| Option | Requirement | Expected revenue (est. at 100k PV/month) | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google AdSense | Review + privacy policy | About US$100–400 | Ad quality is out of your control; low-quality ads can appear and undermine the "simple" positioning |
| EthicalAds / Carbon Ads | Developer / technical audience | About US$200–600 | Thin supply for Taiwan / Hong Kong traffic |
| Direct sponsorship (platforms / producers) | Business development | US$500–5,000 per deal | **Conflict of interest**: sponsors may demand placement |
| Affiliate referral (OTT sign-up / viewing) | Platform partnership | Per conversion | Requires clear disclosure, and only a few eligible platforms in Taiwan |

#### 12.2 The four red lines (non-negotiable)

1. **Never sell ranks or scores**: once it is discovered, the L3 trust layer collapses and the product is worth zero.
2. **No ads above the fold or around the score**: the first screen of the home page and the score area of a title page are permanently ad-free.
3. **Always disclose**: sponsored content and affiliate links must be labelled; the privacy policy must spell out how tracking works.
4. **No intrusive formats**: no interstitials, no pop-ups, no autoplaying video — that is exactly what we criticise mainland apps for.

#### 12.3 Recommendation

- **Year one**: no ads. Build authority through "free + researcher citations + press coverage"; that is the only path that can overtake IMDb.
- **Year two**: if traffic is stable, take one EthicalAds slot (above the footer), or publish a sponsored research report.
- **Long term**: the B2B data API / dashboard (US$500–5,000/month) has far better unit economics than display advertising, and does not hurt the user experience.

> Remember: the value of this site is "saving the user time". Any ad that makes you stay 30 seconds longer dilutes that value.
